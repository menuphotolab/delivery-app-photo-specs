# Notice: what is licensed, and what is not

The repository is released under [CC BY 4.0](./LICENSE). That covers what we
actually made. It is worth being precise about what that is, because a licence
on a reference work can easily overclaim.

## The numbers are facts. Use them freely.

An aspect ratio, a pixel dimension, a file size cap: these are facts about how
someone else's product behaves. **Facts are not ours and are not copyrightable.**
If you want to use the figure "DoorDash item photos are 1400 x 800" in your own
work, you need nothing from us, not even attribution.

We would rather say that plainly than let a licence imply we own something we
do not.

## What CC BY 4.0 actually covers

Three things, all of which took work and none of which are facts:

1. **The written analysis.** Particularly [CONTRADICTIONS.md](./CONTRADICTIONS.md),
   which is original commentary on where these platforms disagree with
   themselves.
2. **The compilation.** Which platforms to include, which figures matter, how
   they are grouped and compared, and the decision to record disagreements
   rather than resolve them.
3. **The structured representation** in [`specs.json`](./specs.json), including
   the schema and the way the cross-platform conflict is modelled.

If you reuse those, attribution is the ask. A link back to this repository is
plenty.

## Quoted platform wording

Some entries quote a platform verbatim, because paraphrasing a requirement can
change it. Those quotations are short, attributed, and carry a source URL and a
date. They are reproduced for identification and commentary. They belong to
DoorDash, Uber Eats, Grubhub, Toast and Square respectively, and no licence we
grant extends to them.
